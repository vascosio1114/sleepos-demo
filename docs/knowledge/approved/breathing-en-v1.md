## Chunks

### Chunk 1 — Paced breathing pattern

A simple paced breathing pattern is to inhale for four seconds, hold briefly, exhale for six seconds, and repeat. Many people find this pattern easy to maintain for two to three minutes.

### Chunk 2 — When to use

Slow breathing can be used at any time of day, but is most often used as a wind-down before sleep, after a stressful event, or during a moment of physical tension. It can be used seated or lying down.

### Chunk 3 — When to stop

If you feel light-headed, anxious, or uncomfortable, stop the practice and breathe normally. The pattern is a tool, not a medical treatment. If symptoms persist, talk with a clinician.
