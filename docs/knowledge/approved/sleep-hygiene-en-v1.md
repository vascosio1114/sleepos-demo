## Chunks

### Chunk 1 — Consistent schedule

Adults who keep a consistent sleep and wake time across all days tend to report better sleep quality than those whose schedules vary. Going to bed and waking at similar times supports the body's circadian rhythm.

### Chunk 2 — Bedroom environment

A quiet, dark, cool, and comfortable bedroom supports sleep onset. Common practical steps include blackout curtains, ear plugs, setting the thermostat to a comfortable range, and limiting bedroom use to sleep and intimacy.

### Chunk 3 — Wind-down and screens

Limiting screen use in the hour before bed and having a short wind-down routine (reading, stretching, dim lighting) is associated with faster sleep onset. Caffeine and alcohol close to bedtime can also delay sleep onset and reduce sleep quality.
