## Chunks

### Chunk 1 — What the reaction-time task measures

The five-trial reaction-time task measures how quickly and accurately a person responds to a simple visual signal. It is one of several inputs to the attention score.

### Chunk 2 — What it does NOT measure

The task is not a clinical cognitive assessment. It is not a measure of intelligence, ADHD, dementia, or any other medical condition. SleepOS does not diagnose, prescribe, or treat any condition.

### Chunk 3 — How to interpret the result

Your score is compared to your own previous attempts, not to a population. A change over time may reflect sleep, focus, or effort. A single result is not a clinical signal.
